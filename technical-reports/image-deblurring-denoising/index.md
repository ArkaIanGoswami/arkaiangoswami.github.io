# MATH 563: Image De-Blurring and De-Noising

**Authors:** Daniel Bensimon, David Fleischer, Arka Ian Goswami, Aariyan Al Mamun  
**Institution:** McGill University  
**Year:** 2026  
**Type:** Graduate Course Technical Report

A study of convex optimization methods for image de-blurring and de-noising, including total-variation regularization, Douglas–Rachford splitting, primal-dual methods, ADMM, and Chambolle–Pock algorithms.
